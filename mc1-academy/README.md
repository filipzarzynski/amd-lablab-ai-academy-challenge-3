# AMD AI League — Mini-Challenge 1 (AMD AI Academy)

Mini-Challenge 1 is the foundational learning challenge of the AMD AI League. Unlike Matches 2 and 3 (which are containerized inference benchmarks), Match 1 is completed through the official AMD AI Academy learning platform and earns **Learning points** toward your total tournament standing.

---

## 4-Step Checklist to Complete Match 1

### Step 1: Join the AMD AI Developer Program
* Register for the official AMD developer program:
  👉 [https://www.amd.com/en/developer/ai-dev-program.html](https://www.amd.com/en/developer/ai-dev-program.html)
* Use the same email address that you registered with on LabLab.ai so your completions can be mapped.

### Step 2: Create an AMD Developer Cloud Account
* Set up your account on the AMD Developer Cloud:
  👉 [https://developer.amd.com/](https://developer.amd.com/)
* This will be used for provisioning GPU compute instances (MI300X and Radeon).

### Step 3: Complete the "Build AI" Learning Pathway
* Access the AMD AI Academy:
  👉 [https://developer.amd.com/amd-ai-academy/](https://developer.amd.com/amd-ai-academy/)
* Enroll in and complete the **"Build AI"** pathway.
* Complete the quizzes and module completions to earn official certification badges and challenge points.

### Step 4: Claim Cloud Credits & Access the Free Hackathon GPU Pool
* Claim your hackathon compute credits.
* Access the free dedicated hackathon JupyterLab GPU pool:
  👉 [https://notebooks.amd.com/hackathon](https://notebooks.amd.com/hackathon)
  * **Resource provided**: 1 GPU per session (CDNA MI300X or RDNA Navi 31) with 48 GiB VRAM.
  * **Quota**: 3 hours of GPU running time refreshed daily.
  * **Persistent storage**: 25 GB persistent storage (remember to save files under `/persistent` or `/workspace` depending on your pod URL).
